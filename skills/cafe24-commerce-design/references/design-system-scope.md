# Commerce design-system scope

## File pages

Use: `00 Cover`, `01 Getting Started`, `02 Foundations`, `03 Components`, `10 COSSET Brand`, `11 COSSET Mobile`, `12 COSSET Desktop`, `20 MAZI Brand`, `21 MAZI Screens`, `90 Archive`.

Keep shared commerce behavior separate from brand appearance. COSSET and MAZI may share component anatomy and state names, but never mix brand colors, typography, imagery, or editorial tone.

## Foundations

- Semantic color roles: canvas, surface, text, muted text, border, accent, on-accent, success, warning, error, overlay.
- Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80.
- Radius: 0, 4, 8, 12, 20, full.
- Mobile content width: 390px reference. Desktop: 1440px reference.
- Touch target minimum: 44x44px.

## Components

Announcement bar, header, mobile navigation, search field, button, product card, price and sale display, sold-out badge, category tile, collection story card, filter and sort control, option selector, quantity control, cart line, popup or bottom sheet, empty state, error state.

## COSSET v1 screens

Mobile and desktop: homepage, product listing, product detail, menu/search, cart, collection/lookbook, coupon/notice. Include normal, sale, sold-out, empty, long-name, open/closed, and error states where relevant.

## Reference roles

- Misha & Puff: immersive editorial hero and brand mood.
- Konges Slojd: product discovery and commerce structure.
- Mini Rodini: collection and lookbook storytelling.

Adapt principles; do not copy trademarks, text, proprietary imagery, or layouts verbatim.
