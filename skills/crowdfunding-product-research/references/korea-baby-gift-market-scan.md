# Korea baby gift market scan — 코셋코셋 접목용

Use when the user asks for 국내 출산/백일/돌 선물로 유명한 제품, 브랜드, or 코셋코셋이 참고할 국내 선물 구성.

## Domestic gift categories that map well to 코셋코셋

1. **출산 기본 세트**
   - Typical components: 배냇저고리, 내의, 손싸개, 발싸개, 모자, sometimes 딸랑이.
   - Representative brands: 오가닉맘, 압소바, 에뜨와, 타티네쇼콜라.
   - Why it works: familiar gift grammar, easy budget bands, low explanation burden.
   - Cossetcosset adaptation: 시그니처 패턴 4종/6종 출산 선물박스.

2. **우주복/바디수트/외출복**
   - Representative brands: 오가닉맘, 밍크뮤, 블루독베이비, 베베드피노 베이비.
   - Why it works: more photo-worthy than consumables; good for 100-day/first-birthday gifts.
   - Cossetcosset adaptation: 백일룩/돌룩/기념촬영룩 sets with bonnet/hairband/socks.

3. **손수건/천기저귀/목욕타월 실용 세트**
   - Representative brands: 밤부베베, 마더케이-adjacent 실사용 gift bundles.
   - Why it works: duplicates are acceptable; high daily use; low size-risk.
   - Cossetcosset adaptation: matching pattern gauze handkerchiefs, bibs, blankets, diaper pouch.

4. **스와들/속싸개/수면조끼**
   - Representative signals: 스와들업 and sleepwear products appear in 출산준비물 discussions.
   - Why it works: strong parent pain point around sleep.
   - Cossetcosset adaptation: 수면조끼 + 블랭킷 + 파우치 set; avoid weighted-sleep claims.

5. **애착인형/딸랑이/치발기/쪽쪽이 클립**
   - Representative brands: 젤리캣, 오가닉맘, 모윰.
   - Why it works: cute visual gift reaction; good add-on for gift boxes.
   - Cossetcosset adaptation: start with low-complexity accessory add-ons (딸랑이, 치발기 스트랩, pacifier clip) rather than hard goods like bottles.

## Productization guidance for 코셋코셋

Recommended first tests:

1. **출산선물 6종 박스**: 바디수트/우주복 + 손수건 3–5장 + 턱받이 + 양말 + 패턴 파우치 + 메시지 카드.
2. **백일/돌 촬영룩 세트**: 원피스 or 상하복 + 보넷/헤어밴드 + 양말 + 선물박스.
3. **손수건+블랭킷 실용 세트**: matching pattern handkerchiefs + blanket + bib + diaper pouch.

Key local-market lesson: domestic gift demand rewards familiar, immediately understandable composition + polished packaging + low failure risk more than novelty. For 코셋코셋, prioritize 감성 패턴 선물박스 over electronics or high-certification gear.
