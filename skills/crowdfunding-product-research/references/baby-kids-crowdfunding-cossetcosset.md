## Research notes / tool patterns

- Kickstarter direct page/browser access may hit Cloudflare. DuckDuckGo Lite/HTML search can still discover Kickstarter URLs and adjacent articles.

## Risk notes

- Weighted infant sleep products: avoid recommending as a product direction. Market examples exist, but US safety/regulatory concern has been raised around weighted infant sleep products. Use alternatives such as lightweight comfort, breathability, soft hand-feel, and seasonal thickness.
- Baby carriers/hip seats: high certification and structural safety burden. Accessories are safer entry points.
- Smart/electronic baby devices: certification, AS, medical/safety claim, and privacy risks. Better as partnership/content than in-house apparel development.

## Recommended 코셋코셋 sequence

1. 4-season sleep vest/sleeping bag line.
2. Swaddle/blanket newborn gift set.
3. Baby-carrier accessory set: drool pads, pacifier clip, teether strap, pouch, winter/rain cover.
4. Curated eco/newborn/100-day/first-birthday gift boxes.
